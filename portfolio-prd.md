## 1. Executive Summary

Kaja's portfolio is a personal webiste to showcase Kaja's product design work. The website is a landing page with seperate pages for each of the work projects.

---

## 2. Objective

- Showcase my work leading projects from 0->1 in tech-startups in San Francisco, India, Belgium, Slovenia, and Germany
- Position myself as a Product Designer who is a generalist, and has a entrepreneurial and leadership mindset
- Include my front end development knowledge and usage of different AI prototyping tools

---

## 3. Audience

1. **Hiring Managers** - Recruit talent at startups
2. **Product Designer** - Fellow designers who may refer or collaborate with me
2. **Founders** - Leaders of the startup looking to hire
3. **Engineers** - Engieers interested in design or looking to hire designers
