# Product Requirements Document

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Kaja's portfolio is a personal webiste to showcase Kaja's product design work.

---

## 2. Objective

- Showcase my work leading projects from 0->1 in tech-startups in San Francisco, India, Belgium, Slovenia, and Germany
- Position myself as a Product Designer who is a generalist, and has a entrepreneurial and leadership mindset
- Include my front end development knowledge and usage of different AI prototyping tools

---

## 3. Audience

1. **Hiring Managers** - Recruit talent at startups
2. **Product Designer** - Fellow designers who may refer or collaborate with me
2. **Founders** - Leaders of the startup looking to hire
3. **Engineers** - Engieers interested in design or looking to hire designers

---

## 4. Content guidelines

- Impact-driven case studies (with short “results” bullets up top)
- Startup logos from past experience (as credibility boosters)
- Clear, confident copy that mirrors how you speak — assertive, not flowery
- Context about the team (size, your role, how you worked with others), timeline, tools used, my role

---

## 4. Design guidelines

- Track and field inspired visual design with the track lanes as the graphic
- When the user scrolls down, the track lanes should intensify to follow the movement of the website as if a runner was running on the lane.
- Small UI interactions (Copy the email address (kaja.skerlj@gmail,com) when pressing C, easter egg information displayed when the mouse moves to a certain place, etc.)
- The website should be inspired by the aesthetic and interaction model in the attached screenshot website.png.
